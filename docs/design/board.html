Lineclear: the design board for Phase 13

The target the Phase 13 steps build to: one mock per view at 390, 768, 1440 and 2560 px, in the railway picture language and the dark graphite theme. Sample data. Every frame is plain HTML and SVG, so a step can copy the measures from it.

The rule of the board: the words stay plain, the pictures carry the railway. One answer per view, and the next tap is next to it.

The parts

One set of SVG parts for every view, the README and the film (U24 builds them, U25 makes them the kit). Nothing else is drawn.

RuleThe measure
ColourGraphite base #0b0b0c, raised #141415. Green is clear (merged, all good), amber waits for you, red only broken. The orange accent is the brand: the mark, the focus ring, the selected tab. It never means a state. A review that is checking is a grey lamp, not amber, so amber always means you.
TypeOne family (U15). The existing scale and no new sizes: 48 the answer, 32 a view title on a phone, 22 a detail title, 18 a section or a card title, 16 body and names, 14 rows, 12 meta. Weight 600 for titles, 500 for names, 400 for the rest. Numbers tabular.
Grid4 px unit. Desktop: rail 232, content up to 1128 on 12 columns with 16 gutters, 40 to 48 padding; a side panel of 400 at 1920 and up. Phone: 16 padding, 48 px targets, bottom bar of five in thumb reach.
SurfacesCards 12 radius, 1 px line #26262a, no shadows except the toast. Rows 44 high on a desktop, 48 on a phone. Hover lifts the fill one step in 120 ms; press scales to 0.97 in 100 ms.

Motion

Motion explains a change and never decorates. Each plays once and stays under 300 ms (U2), keeps input free, and shows only the end state under reduced motion. Two things loop, and only while they last: loading lays the track at U24's time while data is missing, and a lamp that waits on someone blinks slowly while a review checks or something waits for you.

Home

The answer: the state of all work, then the line.

  • The headline is the answer, "Line clear.", and its facts are labels under it: "At work: 7 · Merged today: 3 · Next merge: about 20 min". Home never asks. What waits for you shows as a count on the Inbox tab and an amber lamp on the step in its track (U50).
  • Each task at work is a row: its title, its state in words, how long it has run and its expected finish from the measured pace. A tap opens its step page (U45).
  • One track per project: stations are steps, a train is a task at work, a lamp is a review or a question, the curve is the merge into main. The progress bar and the text list go (F5).
  • Spend and usage leave Home: one line links to Usage.
  • A rail with the tracks from 1024 px (F3). Chat opens as a panel beside any view, and stays open from 1920 px.
  • Phone: a bottom bar of five, the Inbox tab with its count; every target 48 px (F4).

Usage

The facts: each account's use and reset, the free slots, the agents at work, what the work costs. No verdict headline.

  • A view of its own (F9): limits per account with reset times, then machines as sidings with their free slots.
  • Per project and per day: tokens split into cache reads, fresh input and output, so cache hits show as a share of each bar.
  • Tiles: $ per merged step, cache hits in %, compactions, and what the rules saved against a timed AI coordinator, marked estimate with its source.
  • Spend a source does not report says "not reported", never $0. Machines leave Settings and come here (F10).

States

Every view has these. An error never throws away what you saw.

  • Loading lays the track in its final place, so nothing jumps (F19).
  • Stale: the last data stays with a quiet bar, "Not updated since 19:42. Retrying." (F18).
  • Broken is the only red, and says the one command that fixes it.
  • Long names end in an ellipsis on one line, with the full name on hover and focus.
