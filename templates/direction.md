## The owner's direction

Every planner, worker, reviewer and the Dispatcher reads these lines. A plan or a change that goes against one is a
critical finding. A project replaces them with a "## The owner's direction" section in its own docs/idea.md.

- A step is done only when its done-when proof exists. No "not yet recorded".
- Runs go on without the owner: agents decide inside their step. Only a login code, money beyond the budget, or
  something that leaves the machine reaches the owner.
- One small step does one thing and leaves main green. Nothing is pushed or merged by hand.
- Keep what the project already does working; change only what the step needs.
- Plain words everywhere, no AI phrasing.
